# Training Operations · roles and responsibilities

Mock-up, invented for this repository: roles rather than people, so that an answer can say who acts
on it.

| Role | Responsible for | Acts on | Keeps |
|:--|:--|:--|:--|
| Leitung Training Operations | the weekly KPI report, the OKRs, escalations | the five KPIs; a training whose numbers drift | [okrs.md](okrs.md) |
| Coaching Team Lead | assigning coaches, the Ampel's meaning and cadence | the at-risk list each week: who is im Verzug, who shows rot, who has no coach | the Betreuungsliste (the coach Excel) |
| Coach | weekly contact with each participation | their rows on the at-risk list; Ampel and Letzter Kontakt per participation | their rows in the Betreuungsliste |
| Training Operations Admin | enrollment status in the CRM, funding type, planned end, Storno | status changes a coach reports | the CRM record |
| Learning platform team | modules, events and surveys in the LXP | module content | the LXP |
| Data team | this repository: pipeline, model, the weekly numbers | definitions, data-quality findings | README, transform/README.md |

Who to ask: a definition, the Leitung Training Operations decides and the data team documents it
(README § Assumptions) · a wrong status, the Training Operations Admin · a wrong coach or Ampel,
the Coaching Team Lead.
