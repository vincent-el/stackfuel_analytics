# Training Operations · OKRs Q3–Q4 2026

Mock-up, invented for this repository: the shape of the page the weekly report serves, so that a
question can be read in its context. The targets are placeholders, not StackFuel's.

**Objective.** Every participation (Teilnahme) finishes on schedule, and we know early when one
will not.

| Key result | Target | Measured by |
|:--|:--|:--|
| KR1 · share of active participations im Verzug (15 points or more behind schedule) | below 10 % in every training | `report_progress_vs_schedule(reference_date, 15)` |
| KR2 · completion rate of participations whose planned end lies in the quarter | 80 % or more per training and funding type (Förderart) | `report_completion_rates(reference_date)` |
| KR3 · NPS from the Abschlussfeedback | 50 or more per training and quarter | `report_nps_by_quarter(reference_date)` |
| KR4 · every participation has a coach, no coach above 25 active or paused participations | 0 unmatched · at most 25 | `report_coach_load(reference_date)` |

Reviewed in the weekly Training Operations meeting from the weekly report (Monday, Stichtag the
Sunday before). A participation at risk is one that counts against KR1 or shows rot on the
Betreuungsliste; the Coaching Team Lead works that list ([team.md](team.md)).
